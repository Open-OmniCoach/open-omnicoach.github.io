Overview

Connect. Correct. Credit.

Multi-span and reflection data for SFT, then evidence-guided step credit for RL.

How the data is built

Figure 2. OmniCoach-240K data generation pipeline. (a) Hierarchical evidence QA generation: fine-grained audio-visual scripts are linked to a global event timeline, supporting skill-guided MCQ and TG generation followed by quality-aware filtering. (b) Multi-span trajectory generation: using Stage-1 and other-source QA samples, a teacher generates trajectories in the Dynamic Omni-Evidence Harness, which supports joint evidence acquisition across multiple time spans and modalities. (c) Reflection trajectory generation: a verifier diagnoses errors in student rollouts. At confirmed recovery points, a teacher preserves the erroneous draft, adds an evidence-grounded correction and a revised action, and continues the interaction. Both teacher and reflection trajectories are retained only after passing task-level outcome checks. Full resolution (PNG)

Stage 1: Hierarchical Evidence QA Generation

95.7K tasks: 55.3K MCQ and 40.4K TG.

  1. Fine-grained Evidence Generation. Segment-level audio-visual scripts record people, sound sources, and objects with a global entity table. Audiovisual Script Refinement then re-examines each segment to fix sound-source attribution, boundary drift, and missing facts.
  2. Event and Evidence Matching. Scripts are merged into a concise global event timeline. Each event keeps links to its source intervals, so task construction can pick events top-down and expand local evidence bottom-up.
  3. Skill-guided QA Generation. 18 MCQ reasoning skills and one TG skill guide the model to compose events and confounding distractors while recording task-level evidence annotations 𝒜x.
  4. Quality-aware Filtering. Text-only guessable MCQs are dropped. A verifier answers each task from 𝒜x alone and from the full video; the answers must agree. TG requires a one-to-one match with a tIoU above threshold.

Stage 2: Multi-Span Trajectory Generation

A teacher model (Gemini 3.6) interacts with the Dynamic Omni-Evidence Harness. Each trajectory starts with the task, tool specs, timestamped frames covering the whole video at 0.5 FPS (up to 256 frames at 224² pixels), and the full audio track. The teacher may issue up to 32 tool calls. Coarse frames are sampled at 1 FPS, detail frames at 2 FPS; the default longest side is 384 px, with resolution=high up to 1280 px for up to 5 s.

The key extension is get_multi_span, which requests 2–8 ordered intervals in one call, each returning frames, audio, or both. This lets the model compare candidate spans side by side instead of accumulating errors across sequential calls. Trajectories pass an outcome check: correct answer for MCQ, or set-level tIoU plus minimum coverage for TG.

See multi-span retrieval and harness-limit adaptation in the Car launch S4–S5 explorer, and 4-span visual search in the Istanbul S13–S14 explorer / Istanbul video demo.

Stage 3: Reflection Trajectory Generation

17.4K reflection trajectories from failed student rollouts.

  1. Trajectory Collection. Eight rollouts per query are sampled from the Stage-2 SFT student; failed trajectories are selected for diagnosis.
  2. AV-Decoupled Stepwise Verification. A verifier examines key steps in temporal order, checking audio, visual, cross-modal, and agentic support, and stops at the first confirmed error.
  3. Same-Turn Recovery. The student's erroneous draft is preserved, an evidence-grounded correction and revised tool call are added in the same turn, and the trajectory continues to a verified final answer.

Inspect a real student visual hallucination and teacher high-resolution recovery in the Porsche S2 explorer / Porsche video demo.

Method

SEPO: credit useful steps.

Adjust each step’s update by the evidence it adds, while preserving the trajectory advantage’s sign.

Step-level advantage

Evidence gains are standardized within each rollout, then clipped to [−1, 1].

Evidence scoring & optimization

Task-specific evidence utility. The scorer matches the modalities and temporal intervals of retrieved spans to the task’s evidence annotations. For QA, it combines coverage and locality: qm=αcm+(1−α)ℓmq_m=\alpha c_m+(1-\alpha)\ell_mqm​=αcm​+(1−α)ℓm​, with α = 0.5 by default. For temporal grounding, it uses the TimeLens2 grounding reward scaled to [0, 1]. Scores are combined across annotated modalities.

Historical best, then incremental gain. A bounded subset search selects the best-matching retrieved evidence. The utility keeps the historical best score, so redundant or irrelevant retrieval does not lower evidence already acquired. The next step’s gain is the improvement over that best score.

Within-rollout normalization. μ and σ are the mean and standard deviation of the step gains; δ is a positive stability term. An above-average gain gives z > 0. A positive raw gain alone does not guarantee a positive adjustment. The group-relative trajectory advantage comes from outcome and format rewards.

Token updates. Each generated token receives its step’s adjusted advantage in the GSPO-token objective. With λ = 0, SEPO reduces to the GSPO update. Evidence annotations guide training; the agent does not receive them at inference.

Full training recipe
  1. 01

    Multi-span SFT

    Train the base model on teacher-generated multi-span trajectories. Use this first SFT model to collect student rollouts and build reflection trajectories from its failures.

  2. 02

    Mixed-data SFT

    Start again from the same base checkpoint and train on both multi-span and reflection trajectories. This model initializes reinforcement learning.

  3. 03

    Evidence-guided RL

    Optimize the interaction policy with SEPO, combining trajectory-level outcome feedback with the evidence contribution of individual steps.
